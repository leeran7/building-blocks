# Approved direction from user correction

All 18 new characters must look like their Choose Character portraits. Those existing portraits in app/mobile/src/assets/avatars are the identity source of truth. Preserve face/head shape, silhouette, colours, ears/horns/crest, feather/armor structure, and faceted render style. No added Wraith hood or cowl. Wraith sheets are layout/pose references only. Optional Void accents must be restrained and cannot replace source character identity.

The 1024px bust PNGs are faithful upscales of the original 512px portraits, not newly invented designs. The original generation-pack.md is preserved for provenance; its conflicting hood and restricted-palette directions are superseded by this correction.

Pose masters use 4x2 frames. Climb generation uses a 3x2 square-cell grid because the generator does not reliably deliver 6:1; postprocessing assembles six cells into the required horizontal strip. Generated art must pass visual and geometry validation before integration.
